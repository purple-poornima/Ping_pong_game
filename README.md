The logic of ping pong :<br>
                  there are two players with each paddle the players will move their paddles such that they protect the wall from the ball if hit the opponent gets the score <br>
The coding logic of ping pong:<br>
               The loop logic--every games are fully based on the loops where they form one frame ,erase , form another frame ,on and on.<br>
               The player interaction--keys here I coded are "W" to move top and "S" to move bottom.<br>
               The paddles-there are two paddles one is the left which is the AI paddle which just follows the ball and the right is the interactive paddles with W and S keys.<br>
               The ball--the ball moves by just changing the coordinates the more vast the change of the coordinates more the speed<br>
               The background--it is basically a ping pong board<br>
